## 4. Hostile Trap Question Architecture

Module 13 structures every simulated examiner attack using a standardized ten-element forensic rubric:

1. **Examiner Trap Question:** Exact verbal phrasing used by hostile examiner.
2. **Danger Diagnosis:** Why this specific question is lethal if answered casually.
3. **Forbidden Answer:** The naive, colloquial, or marketing response that leads to failure.
4. **Fatal Methodological Flaw:** The precise theoretical or implementation contradiction exposed.
5. **Scientifically Defensible Oral Response:** Structured three-tier response:
   - `SHORT ANSWER:` 15–20 second direct, authoritative, neutral statement.
   - `IF PRESSED:` Deeper technical elaboration citing specific equations or code mechanisms.
   - `BOUNDARY:` Explicit demarcation of model limits, acknowledging necessary wet-lab validation.
6. **Code & Artifact Provenance:** File path, line number, or JSON key establishing ground truth.
7. **Applicable Mathematical / Architectural Boundary:** The formal theorem, constraint, or gate.
8. **Severity Level:** `P0_FATAL_OVERCLAIM`, `P1_MAJOR_EPISTEMIC_SLIP`, or `P2_METHODOLOGICAL_BLUNDER`.
9. **Curriculum Cross-Reference:** Exact mapping to Modules 01–12.
10. **Module 12 Numerical Anchor:** Specific parameter from `NUM-001` through `NUM-090` required.
