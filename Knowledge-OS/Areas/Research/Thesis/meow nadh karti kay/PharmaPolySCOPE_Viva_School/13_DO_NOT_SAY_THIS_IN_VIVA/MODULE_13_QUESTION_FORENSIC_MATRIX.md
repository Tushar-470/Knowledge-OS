## 1. Audit Framework & Evaluation Standard

Each of the 20 simulated viva questions in Deliverable D2 was independently audited against the following nine verification criteria:

1. **Simulation Clarity (SIM):** Is the question clearly framed as an aggressive, simulated viva challenge rather than an author's affirmative assertion?
2. **Concept Distinctness (DIS):** Does the question attack a unique conceptual vulnerability, avoiding redundant overlap with other traps?
3. **Source Grounding (SRC):** Are all cited code files, functions, lines, and validation records authoritative and traceable to the active v2 repository?
4. **Short Answer Technical Soundness (SA):** Is the 15–30 second verbal response concise, direct, non-evasive, and scientifically defensible?
5. **Pressed Answer Technical Depth (PA):** Does the follow-up answer provide exact mathematical derivations, equations, and code mechanisms without hesitation?
6. **Epistemic Boundary Integrity (BND):** Does the boundary clearly state what the model *cannot* compute, preventing causal/physical overreach?
7. **Zero Fabricated Sources (Z-SRC):** Are zero fictitious APIs, functions, classes, or literature citations introduced?
8. **Zero Fabricated Numbers (Z-NUM):** Do all numerical values match `MODULE_12_MASTER_QUANTITATIVE_LEDGER.md` with zero drift?
9. **Zero Mechanism Overclaims (Z-MECH):** Does the response avoid conflating mathematical proxy rankings with physical thermodynamic/solid-state realities?
